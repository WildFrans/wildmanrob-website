Condition Guide

We grade all second-hand firearms honestly so you know exactly what you're getting

Excellent

Near-new condition. Minimal to no visible wear. Fully functional with all original parts. Like buying new at a fraction of the price.

Good

Normal use wear. Fully functional. Bore in good condition. May have minor cosmetic marks but mechanically sound.

Fair

Visible wear and cosmetic marks. Fully functional. Price reflects condition. Great for working farm guns where looks don't matter.
